# DR-002 — OBSIDIAN is not INVAR (no rename)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-31

## Context

Prior artifacts in the NEXTLOGIC tree (e.g. `engineering/err/OBSIDIAN.md`,
`docs/canon/PD-17-H-INVAR-1-RATIFICATION.md`) belong to NEXTLOGIC's own in-flight
work. A global "OBSIDIAN → INVAR" rename, or moving those files into INVAR, was
considered.

## Decision

Do **not** rename OBSIDIAN to INVAR, and do **not** move, copy, or alter those
NEXTLOGIC files. INVAR authors its own documentation from scratch. OBSIDIAN and
any ratification documents remain NEXTLOGIC's, untouched.

## Consequences

- No global identifier rewrite is performed in either repository.
- INVAR's identity is established by its own fresh documents, not by
  re-labelling someone else's.
- The NEXTLOGIC baseline (including those files) must remain byte-identical
  across INVAR's work.
