## New rules, honestly labeled

This release carries two detection rules, and neither changed a single
verdict on held-out data:

- D1 (alpha.9, never tagged): TypeScript `Omit<..., "field">` in a type newly
  introduced at head counts as a removal; Python unannotated class fields
  (`name = X(...)` where the callee ends in Field/Serializer/Column/
  ForeignKey) are read as fields.
- E1 (this release): envelope rule. Keys of dict/object/map literals are
  tracked as paths; a key relocated under a new wrapper at head counts as
  residue unless the consumer mentions a new path segment.

Both specs were frozen and hashed before their held-out batch was sourced
(docs/falsification/D1-SPEC.frozen, E1-SPEC.frozen). On batch 6 and batch 7
the alpha.8, alpha.9 and alpha.10 engines score identically. Each rule fires
only on the past cases it was designed from. Recorded as is.

## The measured content: the corpus

- Batch 7 held out (r1-086..r1-103): 15 real in-scope breaks and 3 controls.
  8 caught, 2 NOT EVALUATED, 5 silent HOLDS; controls 2 clean,
  1 NOT EVALUATED.
- All held-out batches (4 to 7): 30 of 55 in-scope real breaks caught,
  45% missed. The gate for any reliability claim is 20% missed or fewer.
  R1 remains FALSIFIED.
- The one miss mechanism that recurs across batches is a default version or
  format switch at the producer (r1-059, r1-075, r1-099, r1-100). That is
  the next detection target (F1), spec to be frozen before batch 8 is
  sourced.

## Unchanged

from alpha.8.
