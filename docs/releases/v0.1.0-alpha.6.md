INVAR v0.1.0-alpha.6 (prerelease)

## New: stated blind spots (A1)

A contract no longer reports HOLDS when INVAR saw a change it could not
evaluate. It reports NOT EVALUATED, with the site, and exits 4:

- a changed field the consumer mentions outside a read INVAR recognises
  (destructuring, keys the consumer writes, other idioms);
- a field that moved from one type to another;
- nullability is now part of a spec type, so `nullable: true` is a type change.

## invar.lock format 1 frozen

Later releases read format 1 locks and give the same bindings; a new format
gets a new number. See docs/reference/INVAR-LOCK.md.

## Exit codes

0 hold, 1 violated (only), 2 ledger break, 3 error (and LOCK_DRIFT for
`invar lock`), 4 skipped or not evaluated. In the Action, exit 4 does not pass.

## Measured (held-out batch 5: 15 real in-scope breaks, 3 controls)

- Detection unchanged: 7 of 15 caught (53% missed). Across batches 4 and 5,
  13 of 25 caught (48% missed) against a 20% bar: R1 remains FALSIFIED.
- Silent misses (a real break reported HOLDS) fell from 8 to 5 of 15
  (53% to 33%). The A1 rules were frozen before batch 5 was read.
- Cost: 1 of 3 controls is now NOT EVALUATED, and the "moved" rule is noisy
  when producers refactor types or functions. 7 of 68 corpus contracts are
  NOT EVALUATED.

Details: docs/falsification/R1-CORPUS.md.
