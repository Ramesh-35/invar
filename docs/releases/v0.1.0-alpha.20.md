INVAR v0.1.0-alpha.20 (prerelease)

## New: invar demo and invar coverage

`invar demo` replays three real caught breaks (authentik, headscale,
ghostfolio) offline from bytes pinned in demo/ and BLAKE3-verified on
every run, through the stock engine path, with wall time printed as
measured (never promised) and the honest coda. Needs a clone or source
tarball (demo/ is not inside the binary assets). Measured on first runs:
0.3 to 6.6 ms per case (container), 0.4 to 6.1 ms (macOS); reports, not
promises.

`invar coverage [--contract <id>] [--format json]` disclosed per contract:
the static leg in force, what is accused vs stated (stable reason codes),
observed-reality witness counts and thresholds, and the fixed blind spots.
Disclosure, never a verdict.

## Unchanged

Verdict semantics identical to alpha.19; held-out record 38 of 70.
